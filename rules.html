THE RULEBOOK

The rules apply to everyone. Everyone plays by the same standards, and the rules will actually be enforced. The goal is not to remove danger or competition — it is to stop the community from turning into a toxic shitshow.

01

PLAY FAIR

No cheating, hacks, dupes or deliberate abuse of broken mechanics. If something feels like an exploit rather than normal gameplay, report it.

02

PVP STAYS IN THE GAME

Fight, raid, betray and compete. Losing gear, dinos or a base is part of PvP. Personal harassment, threats and targeted abuse are not.

03

EVERYONE FOLLOWS THE SAME RULES

There are no special players who get different standards. The same rules apply whether you are brand new, established, or part of the admin team.

04

NO STRUCTURE SPAM

Build what you need and build something cool, but don't cover the map in unnecessary structures. Remove abandoned junk and keep the server healthy for everyone.

05

NO TRANSFERS

The server is locked off from cross-server transfers. Outside characters, dinos and items cannot be brought in to disrupt progression.

06

NO MODS

No mod stack. Dead Mans Island keeps the experience clean and recognizable as ARK: Survival Ascended.

07

NO WIPES

We are not following the usual wipe-and-start-over model. Player progress matters. Scheduled events will keep things fresh without erasing what people built.

08

NO ADMIN ABUSE

No admin trolling, favouritism or abuse of powers. Admin is active and plays by the same rules as everyone else, stepping in when necessary to keep things running smoothly.

09

REPORT PROBLEMS

Use Discord to report cheating, exploits, serious rule violations or server issues. Give the admin team enough information to actually investigate.

10

BE A DECENT HUMAN

We're looking for a mature player base that actually wants to play ARK. Trash talk and rivalry are fine. Deliberately making the community miserable isn't.

SOMETHING HAPPEN?

REPORT IT.

Bring it to Discord and let the admin team handle it.
