ABOUT DEAD MAN'S ISLAND

Dead Mans Island is a fresh PVP server currently in its testing phase, built for players who want to enjoy ARK without the constant wipe cycle.

We're trying to cut out the unnecessary bullshit by building the server around a real, enforced rulebook that everyone agrees to play by. The goal isn't to remove the danger, competition, or chaos that makes ARK fun — it's to keep the community fair, competitive, and enjoyable without letting it turn into a toxic shitshow.

FEATURES

  • 5X Taming
  • 10X Breeding & Hatching
  • No Transfers
  • No Mods
  • No Wipes
  • Active Admin

OUR VISION

We don't intend to follow the usual “wipe and start over” model. As the server grows, we'll run scheduled server-wide events and activities to keep things fresh without wiping away everything players have built.

We're currently looking for 1–2 players to help get the server established. Early players may have an opportunity to help shape the future moderation team.
